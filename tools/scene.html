TUESDAY · 24 NOVEMBER 2026 · THE DAY’S NEWS, AT A GLANCE
War in Ukraine — Moscow

Putin vows revenge as Ukraine keeps striking his oil plants

President Vladimir Putin said on Tuesday that Kyiv has ignored President Donald Trump’s direct plea to stop hitting Russian refineries — and promised retaliation. Kyiv says it will halt energy strikes only when Moscow does the same. Drone attacks have already cut Russian refining by 45% since late October.

Russian refineries have been hit again and again despite Washington’s appeal. — Illustration
Ukraine — Zaporizhzhia

Night strike kills 2, wounds 40 in Zaporizhzhia

An overnight Russian attack hit an apartment block in the city, killing 2 people and wounding 40, including four children, the regional governor said.

Gaza

Israeli strikes kill Hamas security chief

Israeli airstrikes in Gaza killed Naeem Abu Nahel, director of Hamas’s internal security apparatus, and six others, including two police officers, medics said.

Malaysia — Myanmar

Malaysia to send 6,803 back despite genocide ruling

Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim told parliament 6,803 Myanmar nationals will be repatriated in December — days after the world court found Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya.

Britain

UK warns firms of Russian sabotage threat

The defence ministry and MI5 issued a rare public alert: companies supplying Ukraine’s military face a rising risk of sabotage by operatives acting for Russia.

Poland — NATO

Poland blames Russia for drone in its skies

Warsaw accused Moscow of deliberately violating its airspace after a Russian surveillance drone briefly crossed the border — the latest scare on NATO’s eastern flank.

Korea

Russian jets enter Seoul’s air zone

South Korea said several Russian aircraft flew into its air defence identification zone over waters between the peninsula and Japan, then left. An island shelter alert was briefly issued and lifted.
